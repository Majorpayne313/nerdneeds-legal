Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

When you use Nerd Needs, we collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and unique identifiers

We use your information to:

We may share your information with:

Important: We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. We use encrypted connections such as HTTPS to protect data in transit and take steps to secure stored data.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of certain communications

6. Age Requirement

Nerd Needs is only for users 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

How to Cancel Your Subscription

📱 iOS (Apple App Store)

  1. Open iPhone Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find Nerd Needs and tap it
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

🤖 Android (Google Play)

  1. Open Google Play Store
  2. Tap Menu → Subscriptions
  3. Find Nerd Needs
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription

💰 PayPal

  1. Log in to PayPal.com
  2. Go to Settings → Payments
  3. Click Manage automatic payments
  4. Find Nerd Needs and cancel

What Happens After Cancellation?

  • ✅ Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period
  • ✅ You will not be charged again after cancellation takes effect
  • ✅ Your account is preserved unless you choose to delete it
  • ℹ️ After expiration, access to premium features may end and your account may revert to the free tier

Refund Policy

Important: Refund eligibility depends on your purchase platform and the type of item purchased.

Apple App Store: Request refunds through reportaproblem.apple.com

Google Play: Refund options depend on Google Play policies and timing of the purchase

PayPal or direct support cases: Contact support@nerdneeds.app with your purchase details

Non-Refundable Items

  • Consumed items that have already been used
  • Activated boosts, spotlights, or similar time-based consumables once used
  • Partial subscription periods unless required by law or platform policy
